Parallel Parking¶

This example demonstrates the use of ASSET’s optimal control framework to solve a minimum-time parallel parking problem. A vehicle is maneuvered into a parking space with dimensions only marginally larger than the vehicle itself. The optimization accounts for vehicle dynamics, steering and acceleration limits, roadway boundaries, and collision avoidance.

The example is derived from the parallel parking problem presented in the ICLOCS parallel parking example, which is based on Case 7 in Table 2 of the research paper:

Optimal Control for Automated Parallel Parking.

The initial conditions used here follow the referenced research paper. The resulting maneuver time is compared with the reported paper value of 18.426 seconds.

Problem Description¶

The objective is to find a minimum-time vehicle trajectory that moves a car from an initial position on the roadway into a constrained parallel parking space.

The vehicle must satisfy the following requirements:

  • Begin at a prescribed initial position and orientation.

  • Remain within the roadway and parking-space boundaries.

  • Avoid collisions with the corners of the parking-space entrance.

  • Satisfy velocity, acceleration, steering-angle, jerk, and steering-rate limits.

  • Finish with the vehicle positioned inside the parking space and with zero terminal velocity and acceleration.

The optimization uses a fixed initial state and a free final time. The parking-space geometry is represented using a smoothed step function to provide differentiable boundary constraints.

Vehicle Dynamics¶

The vehicle is modeled as a six-state kinematic bicycle model. The state vector is

\[\mathbf{x} = \begin{bmatrix} x & y & v & a & \theta & \phi \end{bmatrix}^{T},\]

where:

  • \(x,y\) are the vehicle position coordinates.

  • \(v\) is the longitudinal velocity.

  • \(a\) is the longitudinal acceleration.

  • \(\theta\) is the vehicle heading angle.

  • \(\phi\) is the steering angle.

The control vector contains the jerk and steering-angle rate:

\[\mathbf{u} = \begin{bmatrix} u_1 & u_2 \end{bmatrix}^{T}.\]

The equations of motion are

\[\begin{split}\dot{x} &= v\cos(\theta), \\ \dot{y} &= v\sin(\theta), \\ \dot{v} &= a, \\ \dot{a} &= u_1, \\ \dot{\theta} &= \frac{v\tan(\phi)}{l_{\mathrm{axes}}}, \\ \dot{\phi} &= u_2,\end{split}\]

where \(l_{\mathrm{axes}}\) is the distance between the front and rear axles.

The model is implemented by subclassing oc.ODEBase. This allows the vehicle dynamics and geometric constraint functions to be used directly in ASSET’s optimal control phase.

Vehicle Geometry¶

The vehicle is represented as a rectangular body defined by four corners in the vehicle body frame. The corner coordinates are measured relative to the center of the rear axle.

The vehicle dimensions are specified by:

  • l_front: distance from the front axle to the front of the vehicle.

  • l_axes: distance between the front and rear axles.

  • l_rear: distance from the rear axle to the rear of the vehicle.

  • b_width: half-width of the vehicle.

The body-frame corner coordinates are rotated into the inertial frame using the vehicle heading angle. These transformed coordinates are used to construct the roadway and parking-space constraints.

Roadway and Parking-Space Constraints¶

The parking space is defined using its length, width, and the roadway width. The roadway and parking-space boundaries are enforced at all four vehicle corners.

The parking-space boundary is modeled using a hyperbolic tangent approximation of the Heaviside step function:

\[H_k(x) = \frac{1+\tanh(kx)}{2},\]

where \(k\) controls the sharpness of the transition. The parking-space boundary is constructed as

\[F_{\mathrm{slot}}(x) = \left[-H_k(x)+H_k(x-S_L)\right]S_W,\]

where \(S_L\) is the parking-space length and \(S_W\) is the parking-space width.

The smoothing parameter allows the boundary to be represented by a differentiable function, which is useful for gradient-based optimization. The parameter is initially set to a moderate value and increased during the refinement process to provide a closer approximation to the desired parking-space geometry.

Corner Collision Constraints¶

Additional constraints prevent the vehicle from colliding with the upper corners of the parking-space entrance.

These constraints use the areas of triangles formed by the vehicle corners and the two parking-space entrance corners. The sum of the triangle areas is compared with a reference area based on the vehicle dimensions.

The resulting inequalities constrain the vehicle geometry relative to both entrance corners. This provides an additional geometric collision-avoidance condition during the maneuver.

Terminal Constraints¶

At the end of the maneuver, all four vehicle corners must lie within the parking-space width. The terminal longitudinal velocity and acceleration are also constrained to zero:

\[v(t_f)=0, \qquad a(t_f)=0.\]

Together, these conditions require the vehicle to finish inside the parking space in a stationary configuration.

Control and Path Constraints¶

The optimization enforces bounds on the vehicle states and controls. The example uses the following limits:

Quantity

Symbol

Bound

Steering angle

\(\phi\)

\(\pm 33^\circ\)

Velocity

\(v\)

\(\pm 2\ \mathrm{m/s}\)

Acceleration

\(a\)

\(\pm 0.75\ \mathrm{m/s^2}\)

Jerk

\(u_1\)

\(\pm 0.5\ \mathrm{m/s^3}\)

Steering-angle rate

\(u_2\)

Bounded through the curvature-rate constraint

The curvature-rate constraint is defined by

\[\dot{\kappa} = \frac{u_2} {l_{\mathrm{axes}}\cos^2(\phi)}.\]

The absolute value of this quantity is limited to 0.6 in the example.

Optimal Control Formulation¶

The problem is formulated as an ASSET optimal control phase using the LGL5 transcription. The initial trajectory guess is constructed from a sequence of vehicle positions, headings, and times.

The initial guess provides a rough maneuver consisting of several forward and reverse motions. The optimizer then adjusts the trajectory and control history to satisfy the constraints while minimizing the maneuver time.

The objective is

\[\min_{\mathbf{x}(t),\mathbf{u}(t),t_f} \quad t_f.\]

The phase uses block-constant controls and a static smoothing parameter for the parking-space boundary.

The optimization proceeds in two stages:

  1. The initial problem is solved using 50 segments and a smoothing parameter of 75.

  2. The trajectory is refined to 200 segments, the smoothing parameter is increased to 150, and the problem is optimized again using a tighter KKT tolerance.

This refinement procedure improves the representation of the parking-space boundary while increasing the temporal resolution of the trajectory.

Trajectory Propagation and Validation¶

After optimization, the resulting trajectory is extracted from the phase and used to construct an integration table. The vehicle dynamics are then independently reintegrated using an ASSET integrator.

The reintegrated trajectory is sampled at 500 points. The terminal state difference between the optimized trajectory and the reintegrated solution is printed to assess the consistency of the trajectory and the vehicle dynamics.

The maneuver time is also compared against the reference value reported in the research paper:

\[t_{\mathrm{paper}} = 18.426\ \mathrm{s}.\]

Visualization and Animation¶

The example includes plotting and animation utilities to visualize the parking maneuver.

The trajectory visualization displays:

  • The roadway and parking-space boundaries.

  • The vehicle at selected points along the trajectory.

  • The vehicle orientation and position throughout the maneuver.

The animation additionally displays the vehicle body, front and rear axles, wheels, and the path traced by the vehicle. The simulation time is displayed as the maneuver progresses.

The animation can optionally be saved as an MP4 file using Matplotlib’s FFMpegWriter. This requires FFmpeg to be installed and available on the system path.

Running the Example¶

The example requires the following Python packages:

  • NumPy

  • Matplotlib

  • Seaborn

  • ASSET

Seaborn can be installed using:

pip install seaborn

Run the example from the repository using:

python asset_asrl/examples/ParallelParking.py

The main routine constructs the vehicle model, defines the optimal control phase, solves and refines the trajectory, reintegrates the solution, and displays the trajectory and animation.

The complete Python implementation is included directly from the ASSET repository. This ensures that the code displayed in the documentation remains synchronized with the executable example.

 33def corner_location(theta, loc_x, loc_y):
 34    """Rotate a body-frame point by the vehicle heading angle."""
 35    x_rotated = vf.cos(theta) * loc_x - vf.sin(theta) * loc_y
 36    y_rotated = vf.sin(theta) * loc_x + vf.cos(theta) * loc_y
 37    return x_rotated, y_rotated

 40def heaviside(x, k=10):
 41    """Smooth approximation of the Heaviside step function."""
 42    return (1.0 + vf.tanh(k * x)) / 2.0

 45def parking_slot_boundary(x, k, slot_length, slot_width):
 46    """Return the smoothed lower boundary of the parking space."""
 47    return (-heaviside(x, k) + heaviside(x - slot_length, k)) * slot_width

 50def triangle_area(point_a, point_b, point_c):
 51    """Calculate the area of a triangle using its vertex coordinates."""
 52    x1, y1 = point_a
 53    x2, y2 = point_b
 54    x3, y3 = point_c
 55    return abs(x1 * (y2 - y3) + x2 * (y3 - y1) + x3 * (y1 - y2)) / 2.0

 58class CarODE(oc.ODEBase):
 59    """Kinematic bicycle model with vehicle geometry constraints."""
 60
 61    def __init__(self, l_front, l_axes, l_rear, b_width):
 62        """Initialize the vehicle dimensions and equations of motion."""
 63
 64        self.l_front = l_front
 65        self.l_axes = l_axes
 66        self.l_rear = l_rear
 67        self.b_width = b_width
 68
 69        self.area_reference = (l_axes + l_front + l_rear) * 2.0 * b_width
 70
 71        # Vehicle corners in the body frame, measured from the rear axle.
 72        self.corner_a = [l_front + l_axes, b_width]
 73        self.corner_b = [l_front + l_axes, -b_width]
 74        self.corner_c = [-l_rear, b_width]
 75        self.corner_d = [-l_rear, -b_width]
 76        self.corners = [self.corner_a, self.corner_b, self.corner_c, self.corner_d]
 77
 78        state_control = oc.ODEArguments(6, 2)
 79        x, y, velocity, acceleration, theta, phi = state_control.XVec().tolist()
 80        jerk, steering_rate = state_control.UVec().tolist()
 81
 82        x_dot = velocity * vf.cos(theta)
 83        y_dot = velocity * vf.sin(theta)
 84        velocity_dot = acceleration
 85        acceleration_dot = jerk
 86        theta_dot = velocity * vf.tan(phi) / self.l_axes
 87        phi_dot = steering_rate
 88
 89        dynamics = vf.stack([x_dot, y_dot, velocity_dot, acceleration_dot, theta_dot, phi_dot])
 90        super().__init__(dynamics, 6, 2)
 91
 92    def slot_bounds(self, slot_length, slot_width, curb_width):
 93        """Constrain all vehicle corners to remain within the roadway and parking-space boundaries."""
 94
 95        x, y, theta, smoothing = args(4).tolist()
 96        constraints = []
 97
 98        for loc_x, loc_y in self.corners:
 99            rotated_x, rotated_y = corner_location(theta, loc_x, loc_y)
100            corner_x = x + rotated_x
101            corner_y = y + rotated_y
102
103            upper_boundary = corner_y - curb_width
104            lower_boundary = -corner_y + parking_slot_boundary(corner_x, smoothing, slot_length, slot_width)
105
106            constraints.extend([upper_boundary, lower_boundary])
107
108        return vf.stack(constraints)
109
110    def corner_collision_constraints(self, slot_length):
111        """Prevent collisions with the two upper corners of the parking-space entrance."""
112
113        x, y, theta = args(3).tolist()
114        slot_corner_left = [0.0, 0.0]
115        slot_corner_right = [slot_length, 0.0]
116
117        vehicle_corners = []
118
119        for loc_x, loc_y in self.corners:
120            rotated_x, rotated_y = corner_location(theta, loc_x, loc_y)
121            vehicle_corners.append([x + rotated_x, y + rotated_y])
122
123        corner_a, corner_b, corner_c, corner_d = vehicle_corners
124
125        left_areas = [
126            triangle_area(slot_corner_left, corner_a, corner_b),
127            triangle_area(slot_corner_left, corner_c, corner_b),
128            triangle_area(slot_corner_left, corner_a, corner_d),
129            triangle_area(slot_corner_left, corner_d, corner_c),
130        ]
131
132        right_areas = [
133            triangle_area(slot_corner_right, corner_a, corner_b),
134            triangle_area(slot_corner_right, corner_c, corner_b),
135            triangle_area(slot_corner_right, corner_a, corner_d),
136            triangle_area(slot_corner_right, corner_d, corner_c),
137        ]
138
139        constraint_left = self.area_reference - vf.sum(left_areas)
140        constraint_right = self.area_reference - vf.sum(right_areas)
141
142        return vf.stack(constraint_left, constraint_right)
143
144    def final_y_constraints(self):
145        """Constrain all four vehicle corners to lie within the parking-space width."""
146
147        y, theta = args(2).tolist()
148        constraints = []
149
150        for loc_x, loc_y in self.corners:
151            _, rotated_y = corner_location(theta, loc_x, loc_y)
152            constraints.append(y + rotated_y)
153
154        return vf.stack(constraints)
155
156    def curvature_rate(self):
157        """Return the rate of change of vehicle curvature."""
158
159        phi, steering_rate = args(2).tolist()
160        return steering_rate / (self.l_axes * vf.cos(phi) ** 2)

163def make_state(x, y, theta_degrees, time):
164    """Construct an ASSET state-control vector for the initial guess."""
165
166    state = np.zeros(9)
167    state[0] = x
168    state[1] = y
169    state[4] = np.deg2rad(theta_degrees)
170    state[6] = time
171
172    return state

175def rotation_matrix(angle):
176    """Return a two-dimensional rotation matrix."""
177    return np.array([[np.cos(angle), -np.sin(angle)], [np.sin(angle), np.cos(angle)]])
178

180def transform_point(position, heading, local_point):
181    """Transform a body-frame point into the inertial frame."""
182    return np.asarray(position) + rotation_matrix(heading) @ np.asarray(local_point)

185def vehicle_body_points(vehicle, state):
186    """Return the vehicle body polygon in inertial coordinates."""
187
188    position = state[0:2]
189    heading = state[4]
190    local_points = [vehicle.corner_a, vehicle.corner_c, vehicle.corner_d, vehicle.corner_b]
191
192    return np.array([transform_point(position, heading, point) for point in local_points])

195def wheel_points(vehicle, state, axle_position, steering_angle=0.0):
196    """Return the four corners of a wheel in inertial coordinates."""
197
198    position = state[0:2]
199    heading = state[4]
200    wheel_length = 0.30
201    wheel_width = 0.12
202
203    local_points = np.array([
204        [wheel_length, wheel_width],
205        [-wheel_length, wheel_width],
206        [-wheel_length, -wheel_width],
207        [wheel_length, -wheel_width],
208    ])
209
210    rotated_points = (rotation_matrix(steering_angle) @ local_points.T).T
211    wheel_center = np.asarray(axle_position)
212
213    return np.array([transform_point(position, heading, wheel_center + point) for point in rotated_points])

216def vehicle_wheel_geometry(vehicle, state):
217    """Return the front and rear wheel polygons."""
218
219    axle_width = vehicle.b_width / 1.5
220    rear_centers = [[0.0, axle_width], [0.0, -axle_width]]
221    front_centers = [[vehicle.l_axes, axle_width], [vehicle.l_axes, -axle_width]]
222
223    rear_wheels = [wheel_points(vehicle, state, center) for center in rear_centers]
224    front_wheels = [wheel_points(vehicle, state, center, state[5]) for center in front_centers]
225
226    return rear_wheels, front_wheels

229def vehicle_axle_geometry(vehicle, state):
230    """Return the rear and front axle line segments."""
231
232    position = state[0:2]
233    heading = state[4]
234    axle_width = vehicle.b_width / 1.5
235
236    rear_axle = np.array([
237        transform_point(position, heading, [0.0, axle_width]),
238        transform_point(position, heading, [0.0, -axle_width]),
239    ])
240
241    front_axle = np.array([
242        transform_point(position, heading, [vehicle.l_axes, axle_width]),
243        transform_point(position, heading, [vehicle.l_axes, -axle_width]),
244    ])
245
246    return rear_axle, front_axle

249def plot_slot(ax, smoothing, slot_length, slot_width, curb_width, x_limits=(-8.0, 8.0)):

252    x_values = np.linspace(x_limits[0], x_limits[1], 2000)

259    ax.fill_between(x_values, y_values, 0.0, color="green", alpha=0.2, label="Parking space")
260    ax.plot(x_limits, [curb_width, curb_width], color="red", linewidth=1.5)

395    """Construct, solve, and visualize the parallel parking problem."""
396
397    # Parking-space dimensions.
398    slot_length = 5.0
399    slot_width = 2.0
400    curb_width = 3.5
401
402    # Vehicle dimensions.
403    l_front = 0.839
404    l_axes = 2.588
405    l_rear = 0.657
406    b_width = 1.771 / 2.0

408    vehicle = CarODE(l_front, l_axes, l_rear, b_width)

411    max_steering_angle = np.deg2rad(33.0)
412    max_velocity = 2.0
413    max_acceleration = 0.75
414    max_jerk = 0.5
415    max_curvature_rate = 0.6
416
417    min_x = -10.0
418    max_x = 7.5
419
420    # Initial conditions from the reference paper.
421    initial_x = -5.14
422    initial_y = 1.41
423    initial_heading_degrees = 13.18
424
425    initial_state = make_state(initial_x, initial_y, initial_heading_degrees, 0.0)
426
427    # Initial trajectory guess.
428    guess_1 = make_state(0.0, initial_y, 0.0, 5.0)
429    guess_2 = make_state(5.5, initial_y, 10.0, 10.0)
430    guess_3 = make_state(1.0, -0.5, 20.0, 15.0)
431    guess_4 = make_state(1.0, -1.0, 0.0, 25.0)
432
433    trajectory_guess = [initial_state, guess_1, guess_2, guess_3, guess_4]
434
435    # Display the initial trajectory guess.
436    plot_trajectory(vehicle, trajectory_guess, 1000.0, slot_length, slot_width, curb_width)
437    plt.show()
438
439    # Optimization and mesh-refinement parameters.
440    initial_segments = 50
441    refined_segments = 200
442    initial_smoothing = 75.0
443    refined_smoothing = 150.0
444
445    # Construct the optimal control phase.
446    phase = vehicle.phase("LGL5", trajectory_guess, initial_segments, True)
447    phase.setStaticParams([initial_smoothing])
448    phase.setControlMode("BlockConstant")
449
450    # Fix the initial vehicle state.
451    phase.addBoundaryValue("First", range(0, 7), initial_state[0:7])
452
453    # Enforce roadway and parking-space boundaries.
454    phase.addInequalCon("Path", vehicle.slot_bounds(slot_length, slot_width, curb_width), [0, 1, 4], [], [0])
455
456    # Require the final vehicle corners to lie within the slot.
457    phase.addInequalCon("Last", vehicle.final_y_constraints(), [1, 4])
458
459    # Require zero terminal velocity and acceleration.
460    phase.addBoundaryValue("Last", [2, 3], [0.0, 0.0])
461
462    # State and control bounds.
463    phase.addLUVarBound("Path", 0, min_x, max_x)
464    phase.addLUVarBound("Path", 2, -max_velocity, max_velocity)
465    phase.addLUVarBound("Path", 3, -max_acceleration, max_acceleration)
466    phase.addLUVarBound("Path", 5, -max_steering_angle, max_steering_angle)
467    phase.addLUVarBound("Path", 7, -max_jerk, max_jerk)
468
469    # Bound the rate of change of curvature.
470    phase.addLUFuncBound("Path", vehicle.curvature_rate(), [5, 8], -max_curvature_rate, max_curvature_rate)
471
472    # Prevent collisions with the parking-space entrance corners.
473    phase.addInequalCon("Path", vehicle.corner_collision_constraints(slot_length), [0, 1, 4])
474
475    # Keep the initial smoothing parameter fixed during the first solve.
476    phase.addValueLock("StaticParams", [0])
477
478    # First optimization.
479    phase.addDeltaTimeObjective(1)
480    phase.optimizer.set_BoundFraction(0.995)
481    phase.optimizer.set_MaxIters(2000)
482    phase.optimizer.set_PrintLevel(1)
483    phase.solve_optimize()
484
485    # Mesh refinement and second optimization.
486    phase.refineTrajManual(refined_segments)
487    phase.subVariable("StaticParams", 0, refined_smoothing)
488    phase.optimizer.set_KKTtol(1.0e-8)
489    phase.optimize()
490
491    # Extract optimized trajectory.
492    trajectory = phase.returnTraj()
493    trajectory_table = phase.returnTrajTable()
494
495    # Independently reintegrate the optimized trajectory.
496    integrator = vehicle.integrator(0.1, trajectory_table)
497    reintegrated_trajectory = integrator.integrate_dense(trajectory[0], trajectory[-1][6], 500)
498
499    # Report maneuver time and terminal-state consistency.
500    final_time = trajectory[-1][6]
501    reference_time = 18.426
502
503    print()
504    print("=" * 60)
505    print("PARALLEL PARKING OPTIMIZATION RESULTS")
506    print("=" * 60)
507    print(f"ASSET maneuver time:      {final_time:.6f} s")
508    print(f"Reference maneuver time:  {reference_time:.6f} s")
509
510    time_difference = final_time - reference_time
511    time_difference_percent = 100.0 * time_difference / reference_time
512
513    print(f"Time difference:          {time_difference:.6f} s")
514    print(f"Relative time difference: {time_difference_percent:.4f} %")
515
516    terminal_state_error = trajectory[-1][0:7] - reintegrated_trajectory[-1][0:7]
517
518    print()
519    print("Optimized versus reintegrated terminal-state difference:")
520    print(terminal_state_error)
521    print("Terminal-state difference norm:", np.linalg.norm(terminal_state_error))
522    print("=" * 60)
523
524    # Plot optimized trajectory.
525    plot_trajectory(vehicle, trajectory, refined_smoothing, slot_length, slot_width, curb_width)
526    plt.show()
527
528    # Animate the reintegrated trajectory.
529    animate_trajectory(vehicle, reintegrated_trajectory, refined_smoothing, slot_length, slot_width, curb_width, save=False)
